# [H. Deadpool's Recruitment](https://codeforces.com/gym/588606/problem/H)

---
Deadpool is on a mission to assemble the X-Force team, but he decided to make it a little competitive (and totally chaotic). The challenge? Grow a magical plant under tricky cosmic conditions. Aspiring members of the X-Force must prove their strategic brilliance and creativity in this two-phase competition.Competition Details:  The competition has n participants, each trying for a spot in the X-Force. The magical plant grows in two phases:   The first phase lasts for t1 units of time.  The second phase lasts for t2 units of time.   Cosmic Chaos Alert: After the first phase, the magical plant's size drops by k percent due to interference from Deadpool's random cosmic experiments. Each participant chooses two distinct growth speeds:   Speed ai for one phase and speed bi for the other phase.  Participants can decide the order in which to use these speeds, but each speed can only be used once.   Deadpool is keeping a close eye to ensure no foul play or perhaps, to add some mischief of his own.  Each participant aims to maximize the final height of their magical plant after both phases. Your mission is to calculate the maximum possible plant height for each participant and create a final results table, adhering to the rules:Participants with higher final plant heights appear earlier in the table. If two participants achieve the same plant height, the participant with the smaller number (index) appears earlier.Deadpool needs you help your time is now.

### Input
InputThe input consists of:A single line with four integers:n: the number of participants (1≤n≤1000)t1: the duration of the first phase (1≤t1≤1000)t2: the duration of the second phase (1≤t2≤1000)k: the percentage reduction in size after the first phase due to cosmic interference (1≤k≤100).The next n lines, each containing two integers ai and bi (1≤ai,bi≤1000), representing the two growth speeds chosen by the ith participant.

### Output
OutputPrint the final results table with n lines. Each line should contain:The maximum height of their magical plant, The index number of the participant, formatted to exactly two decimal places.
