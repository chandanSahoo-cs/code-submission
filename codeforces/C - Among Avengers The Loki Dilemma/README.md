# [C. Among Avengers : The Loki Dilemma](https://codeforces.com/gym/588606/problem/C)

---
Loki, the God of Mischief, has enchanted n−1n−1 Avengers to spread chaos. He then himself disguises as an avenger and hides among them (So , now there are n avengers) . Some of them, still loyal to their cause, tell the truth, while others, influenced by Loki's magic, lie. Loki himself may lie or tell the truth .Dr. Strange, trying to uncover Loki's influence , asked each of the Avengers the following question:  "Which one of you is Loki ?" Each Avenger i responded with one of the following:   +j: "Avenger j is Loki."  −j: "Avenger j is not Loki" It is possible for an Avenger to make statements about themselves (j=i).Dr. Strange, using the Eye of Agamotto knows that exactly m of these n Avengers are telling the truth , and all other answers are lie. Your task is to help Dr. Strange by determining which Avenger was being truthful and who was telling lie.

### Input
InputThe input consists of the following:   The first line contains two integers n and m (1≤n≤105 , 0≤m≤n) — the total number of Avengers (including disguised Loki) and the number of Avengers who are telling the truth.  The next n lines contain the Avengers' answers:The i-th line contains either "+j" (without the quotes), if the i-th Avenger says that the j-th Avenger is Loki , or "−j" (without the quotes), if the i-th Avenger says that the j-th Avenger is Loki. Here, j is an integer such that 1≤j≤n. It is guaranteed that at least one Avenger exists, such that if he/she is Loki, then exactly m Avengers told the truth.

### Output
OutputPrint n lines. Line i should contain "Truthful" (without quotes) if Avenger i is certain to be truthful , "Enchanted" (without quotes) if Avenger i is certain to be telling lie , "Agamotto failed" (without quotes) if it is not possible to determine the status of Avenger i based on the given information.
NoteTest Case1 : There is only one Avenger, and he claims he is Loki. Dr. Strange knows no Avenger is telling lie. Thus, this Avenger is truthful. Test Case2 : There are three Avengers and each one denies him being Loki. Dr. Strange knows that only two of them are telling the truth. Any one of them can be Loki, so we don't know for any of them, whether this Avenger is telling the truth or not.Test Case3 : The second and the fourth Avenger defend the first and the third one. But only one is telling the truth, thus, the first or the third one is Loki. Both of them can be Loki, so the second and the fourth one can either be lying or telling the truth. The first and the third one are lying for sure as they are blaming the second and the fourth one.
